Brazilian RoadBench: Structured L2+/ADAS Scenario Interpretation under Brazilian Portuguese Variation

Willams de Lima Costa1, João Marcelo Teixeira1, Veronica Teichrieb1
1Voxar Labs, Centro de Informática, Universidade Federal de Pernambuco, Brazil
Accepted at Latin.Science 2026 · Proceedings publication forthcoming
Latin.Science Dataset Code

Brazilian RoadBench pairs 100 reviewed canonical scenarios with 100 Brazilian Portuguese variants that share the same reference labels.

Abstract

Large language models can support driver-facing explanations, fleet reports, and road-event triage, but their reliability under Brazilian Portuguese variation remains unclear. We introduce Brazilian RoadBench, a text-only benchmark for structured L2+/ADAS scenario interpretation. The benchmark contains 100 reviewed canonical Brazilian road scenarios and 100 paired Brazilian Portuguese variants, each mapped to reference labels for road context, actors, hazard type, risk level, ADAS function, recommended action, and a short explanation. We evaluate Portuguese-specialized API models, compact local open-weight models, and Portuguese/Brazilian open-weight models. Sabiá-4 achieves the best overall structured score, followed by Sabiazinho-4, while Qwen3-4B q4 is the strongest local open-weight baseline. Results show that Brazilian variants do not generally collapse aggregate performance, but structured-output compliance, risk calibration, and action/ADAS-function selection remain key bottlenecks. Brazilian RoadBench provides a focused benchmark for studying the trade-off between Portuguese-specialized API performance and reproducible local open-model deployment.

Access to data

Brazilian RoadBench is released under CC BY 4.0. The recommended evaluation file contains 200 prompts: 100 reviewed canonical scenarios and 100 paired Brazilian Portuguese variants.
